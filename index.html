Selected projects

Three projects spanning product design and mobility, one still in progress.

Plate 01

Aquatica

A floor lamp developed in collaboration with lighting company Marioni, born from the meeting of material experimentation and research into light. Ceramic is the project's core material: worked to achieve articulated, irregular surfaces, it modulates and refracts light in a soft, enveloping way; the metal base supports and balances the ceramic element in a solid, essential structure. Inspiration from algae becomes a design principle: just as light is fragmented and transformed by organic forms underwater, Aquatica interprets light as a living material, capable of deep reflections and shifting perceptions.

Product design Ceramics Marioni
Plate 02

Rodeo

A three-wheeled cargo bike designed around its central element: the removable cargo box. Positioned between the two front wheels, the box is conceived as an independent module that can be detached from the bike and used on its own, simplifying loading and unloading and adapting the vehicle to different use cases. The box turns the cargo bike from a single vehicle into a modular system — more flexible and better suited to contemporary urban logistics.

Product design Mobility Modular system

About

My name is Riccardo Dami, I'm 25 years old, and I'm a product & graphic designer with a deep interest in the world of mobility. I observe objects to understand their structure, logic and potential: to me, designing means turning real needs into more fluid, intuitive experiences.

I believe in design that's essential yet alive, able to improve how we move through and interact with the spaces we cross every day. I look for solutions that bring together function, aesthetics and usability in a clear, contemporary language — objects and systems built to simplify, but also to spark new possibilities.

Education

My education began at the traditional scientific-track liceo of I.S.I.A. Isaac Newton, which I attended from 2014 to 2019. In 2021 I enrolled in the Industrial Design degree (L4) at the University of Florence, graduating in 2024.

Experience

I took part in the FirenzeFormaContinua workshop organized by DIDA CommunicationLAB, and in "La scuola del non sapere" in Milan, promoted by Civic City together with Ruedi Baur. I worked on the Cargo Bike VAI per B Cargo project as part of a call by Poste Italiane, and took part in the Codesigning Future Space Exploration Workshop at Engineering LAB. My work has also been featured in "Progettare un quartiere sano," edited by Rinaldi and Setola.
