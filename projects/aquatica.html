Product design Ceramics Marioni

Aquatica

2025

A floor lamp developed in collaboration with lighting company Marioni, born from the meeting of material experimentation and research into light. Ceramic is the project's core material: worked to achieve articulated, irregular surfaces, it modulates and refracts light in a soft, enveloping way; the metal base supports and balances the ceramic element in a solid, essential structure.

Inspiration from algae becomes a design principle: just as light is fragmented and transformed by organic forms underwater, Aquatica interprets light as a living material, capable of deep reflections and shifting perceptions.
