Product design Mobility Modular system

Rodeo

2024

A three-wheeled cargo bike designed around its central element: the removable cargo box. Positioned between the two front wheels, the box is conceived as an independent module that can be detached from the bike and used on its own, simplifying loading and unloading and adapting the vehicle to different use cases.

The box turns the cargo bike from a single vehicle into a modular system — more flexible and better suited to contemporary urban logistics.
